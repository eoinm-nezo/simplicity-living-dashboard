Each element carries three numbers: the product carbon locked in at the factory gate (A1–A3), the Green Star default for getting it to site and building it in, and what Simplicity actually measures for the same work. The default is the honest industry starting point; the measured figure is the challenge to it. Open any element to see both calculated line by line.
