About the Benchmark

The Simplicity Living Benchmark tracks seven aspects of our apartment buildings. We assess each against published targets and supporting evidence, so we can identify improvements and track progress from project to project.

It measures seven selected outcomes; it is not a comprehensive sustainability assessment, regulatory approval or third-party certification. Each module is scored out of 20 and stands on its own merit, calculated upward from the evidence. Open Module Breakdown for any project to see exactly how each point is earned, what the next target is, and where our buildings sit.

Pilot methodology (v0.2). The numeric targets are calibration candidates for back-testing, not approved rules or an external benchmark. Project data shown here is placeholder, pending real evidence.
